## Scope and evidence

Workforce Compliance is registered as `attendance-monitoring`. Its primary surfaces are Attendance, Time Logs, Requests & Approvals, Policies, Audit Trail, and Workforce Logs. The QA boundary also includes their direct contracts with People & HR, notifications, Calendar, and Staff Workload. Use the router mounts and web route policy as entry points, then trace services, data, and tests. Treat those as deferred requirements. Employee status-driven Head handoff in People & HR is a separate implemented workflow. ## Role and visibility matrix

Department Head is a current department assignment for eligible `STAFF`. Admin/HR denotes `ADMIN`. Personal attendance and request creation require an active, non-retired Staff or Admin account with a linked, persisted Employee. Clock-in additionally requires Employee status `ACTIVE`. Leave submission explicitly denies `INACTIVE`. Test account status, employment status, role, and department membership as independent inputs. `USER` (Client), anonymous, disabled, and retired identities are denial fixtures. Use separate browser contexts per role and verify page access, interactions, notification deep links, detail affordances and reload consistency. ## Issue tracking and scope exit

Use case IDs such as `WC-03-C01` and finding IDs such as `WC-03-F01`. Track them in the later audit record for the owning scope. Cross-reference the same finding from dependent scopes. Each finding should capture severity, status, role and scope, preconditions and fixture IDs, observed result, expected result and its source, affected surfaces, evidence, coverage dependency, and retest result. Keep evidence free of credentials and real employee data.

| Severity | Criteria                                                                                                                       |
| -------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Critical | Cross-scope disclosure or unauthorized mutation, irrecoverable time/history loss, or systemic calculation corruption.          |
| High     | Incorrect approval/denial, materially wrong totals or dates, lost or duplicate records, or broken recovery in a core workflow. |
| Medium   | Scoped reporting, usability or recoverable notification issue with bounded impact.                                             |
| Low      | Presentation or documentation discrepancy with no functional effect.                                                           |

Use statuses **Planned**, **Confirmed**, **Needs decision**, **Ready for retest**, **Verified**, and **Deferred**. Record outcomes for applicable roles, terminal states and negative cases before closing a scope. Carry unresolved findings into integration with evidence and a stated impact. Phase 1 assigns no confirmed defect IDs.
